A note on the proof

Every screen on this page is drawn. Nothing is a screenshot going stale in a folder and nothing is an embedded page, which is why none of it stutters. The published work is set the way it actually reads, so the definition card in the brief and the moment the lesson turns around and argues with itself are here rather than one click away. Every private tool is rebuilt in its real shape from its real interface, with invented numbers, and the panel says so on its face.

Not here: anything belonging to an employer, a client or a classmate, and anything that would identify a person. Some of the best work is in that category and stays there.
