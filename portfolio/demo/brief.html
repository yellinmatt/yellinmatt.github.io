A Munich court rules Suno infringed German copyrights
The ruling turns on whether training on catalogue recordings is reproduction, and the court held that it is. The remedy is narrower than the finding: damages, not an injunction, and only for the German catalogue.

What it changes: every model trained on licensed-adjacent audio now carries a European exposure it did not have on Wednesday, and the licensing conversation that stalled in March restarts with a number attached.
