Nothing happened, which is the report

The contribution went out on schedule for the thirty-first consecutive week and was allocated to policy without discretion.

Drift closed the week at 1.4 percent, well inside the five percent threshold, so no rebalancing was required and none was performed. That sentence is the whole of the week's activity, and writing it down is the point: a record that only appears when something happens is a record that flatters itself.

One gate fired. A position in the capped sleeve appreciated past its ceiling on Thursday and the rule trimmed it back on Friday rather than letting a good week rewrite the policy. That is the entire purpose of a cap, and it is worth noting plainly that the trim felt wrong at the time. It usually does. The cap exists for the weeks when the judgement in the room is worse than the judgement written down six months earlier.

Written before the numbers were looked at a second time, which is the only way the tone stays honest.
