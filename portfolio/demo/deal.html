Where accounting becomes cash

Working capital true-up takes the softest numbers on the balance sheet, reserves, accruals and cutoff, and settles them in cash months after the handshake. The figure is not read off the sheet; it is built by applying a negotiated methodology to it.

1.4 Estimated Closing Working Capital shall be calculated in accordance with the Accounting Principles set forth on Schedule 1.4, which shall control in the event of any conflict with GAAP. The order of precedence is the whole negotiation. Schedule 1.4 beats GAAP, so whoever drafts the schedule sets the price.

Three months later the buyer recalculates on its own methodology, and the gap between the two is a wire transfer in one direction or the other.
