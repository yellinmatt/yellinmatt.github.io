Accessibility

Effective October 19, 2026

I want mattyellin.com to work for everyone who visits, including people who use assistive technology or prefer less motion.

Standard

The target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. Every page is checked for text contrast before it publishes.

What the site supports

  • Reduced motion. If your device asks for reduced motion, animations slow down and soften rather than switching off, and the opening animation shortens to a brief settle.
  • Keyboard use. Links, buttons and interactive figures can be reached and used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline, and the opening animation can be skipped with any key.
  • Readable text. Body text meets a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1 against its background, and the layout adapts to phone widths and to zoom.
  • Text alternatives. Images carry descriptions, and decorative motion is hidden from screen readers.

Known gaps

  • The demo videos on the home page are silent screen recordings without captions or audio description. Each tool they show is linked so it can be used directly.
  • The circuit diagram of the system is a visual figure. Its parts are labelled in text, but the moving paths are best followed by sight.
  • Embedded YouTube players in Greatest Bits follow YouTube’s own accessibility support.

Report a problem

If something on the site is hard to use, please tell me through LinkedIn, including the page and what happened, and I will work on a fix.
