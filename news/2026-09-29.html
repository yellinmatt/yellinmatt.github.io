Anthropic’s prospectus: $4.59 billion of revenue, an $8.06 billion operating loss, and $518 billion of compute already promised

Anthropic’s registration statement went public on Monday, the first audited look at what a frontier laboratory costs to run. Revenue for 2025 was $4.59 billion against $386 million the year before. The operating loss was $8.06 billion on about $13 billion of expenses, of which took $7.33 billion, or 58 percent. Year-end cash was $20.28 billion. Second-quarter revenue this year was $11.5 billion, more than the whole of 2025. The $41.97 billion GAAP net loss is a different animal, driven largely by a rather than money leaving the building.

Close to a third of the document is risk factors, and not the usual ones. The filing addresses models resisting shutdown, concealing and manipulating, behaviour resembling blackmail, and risk to the continued existence of humanity, which has not appeared in an American before. Two customers were about a quarter of 2025 revenue. Future compute and cloud obligations run to roughly $518 billion, with Google, SpaceX and Nscale named among the counterparties. The same morning, the company shipped Claude Sonnet 5.5 at $2 and $10 per million tokens.

Set the $518 billion against $20.28 billion of cash and an $8.06 billion annual operating loss and the offering explains itself: the money is not for research, it is for a already signed. Listing after the November midterms at the reported $2 trillion target asks the public market to roughly double the $965 billion mark set in May’s Series H, and to read all of the above as intends it.

The Islanders sell about 15 percent at a $3 billion valuation, six times the 2014 purchase price, subject to NHL approval

Sportico reported on Monday, citing three people familiar with it, that the Islanders have agreed to sell about 15 percent of the franchise at a $3 billion valuation, with one investor reported to take 10 percent. The club had earlier named the venture investor David Shuman as a new minority holder; the other buyers are not identified. Scott Malkin remains controlling owner. He and Jon Ledecky bought the team in 2014 for $485 million. The sale needs approval.

The mark is the story. Sportico valued the club at $1.93 billion in 2025, fourteenth of 32. At $3 billion it ties Boston for fourth, behind Toronto at $4.25 billion, the Rangers at $3.65 billion and Montreal at $3.3 billion. UBS Arena, opened in November 2021 at a cost of at least $1 billion, is co-owned by the team, and Sterling Equities. The team missed the playoffs last season at 43-34-5.
