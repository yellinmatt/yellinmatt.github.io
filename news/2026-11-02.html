The core demands, per AFP, are an end to the war on all fronts, the release of frozen assets, relief on oil sanctions and an end to the . Axios reported, via Anadolu, that Prime Minister Netanyahu pushed in a Saturday call for strikes right after Tuesday’s midterms and that Trump preferred to let diplomacy run. An Israeli source called the call “tough,” a U.S. source “direct.” Trump called it “a very good talk.”

Ukraine evacuated 167 more people on Sunday, including 22 children, from a town that held about 2,000 civilians when the truce began, by a that needed Russian cooperation.

Why it mattersThe of the war produced an evacuation channel and nothing else, as winter grid attacks begin and interceptor stocks run short.
