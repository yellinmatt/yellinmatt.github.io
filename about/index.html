I publish something every morning, and I build the tools I could not find.

The publishing began as a discipline rather than an ambition, on the theory that a thing which has to go out at six is a thing you actually finish. Four of them run now. Two of them go out before I wake up.

The rest is software written for an audience of one. I am a JD/MBA candidate, which is not the obvious route into building things, but the tools I wanted did not exist and making them turned out to be quicker than continuing to want them. None of it is for sale, and most of it is running right now.
