Miami, FloridaWhere all of it is written

Every project, and where its build actually stands today

In prose, since you scrolled

The publishing started as a discipline rather than an ambition, on the theory that a thing which has to go out at six is a thing you actually finish. Four of them run now, and two go out before I wake up. One is a daily newspaper, one is a lesson a day toward a general education, one is a two-year curriculum in corporate law, and one is an archive of comedy indexed so a bit you half remember can be found again.

The rest is software written for an audience of one. Law school is not the obvious route into building things, but the tools I wanted did not exist and making them turned out to be quicker than continuing to want them. None of it is for sale, and most of it is running right now.
