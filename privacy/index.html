Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

This page explains what mattyellin.com collects when you visit and what it does not. The short version is that the site has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies of its own.

What the site collects

Nothing that identifies you. There are no sign-ups, forms, comment boxes or analytics scripts, and the site sets no cookies.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details when your browser loads a page, and may keep them in its own logs for security and operations. GitHub’s handling of that data is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement. The domain’s DNS is managed through Cloudflare, which answers the lookup for the address but does not sit in front of the site or see page traffic.

Third parties your browser contacts

  • Google Fonts. Pages load their typefaces from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details when it serves the font files. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • Wikimedia Commons. Some images in Well-Versed and The Morning Brief load directly from Wikimedia, which receives a standard request when they display.
  • YouTube. Greatest Bits embeds clips from YouTube. A clip contacts YouTube only when it loads or plays, and YouTube’s own privacy terms then apply.

Storage in your own browser

A few tools remember your choices on your device using your browser’s local storage: the Miami Law Course Planner keeps your schedule, Train keeps its log, and some pages remember display preferences or whether you have already seen the opening animation. This data stays in your browser, is never sent to the site, and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this page changes, the effective date above will change with it. Questions can be sent to me through LinkedIn.
