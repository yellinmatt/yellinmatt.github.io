My pick is A. The card law says the hero is the only thing open on a tab, so B needs a written exception and A needs none. A also puts the number where Nike puts it emotionally, underneath the ask rather than in front of it, so it reads as the thing you are about to add to. C is the truest Nike Run Club posture but it makes the odometer compete with the level ring, and the ring is the one thing this tab knows that no other tab does.
